# DER strand math audit (2026-11-26)

Checked against Bergou et al. 2008, "Discrete Elastic Rods", plus the mass-spring inextensibility constraint. Every finding below is backed by a finite-difference (FD) check against its energy. The reference formulas match FD to about 1e-10. The scratch programs live on branch `worktree-agent-a71708594332c6ce2` (commit 383b64d), under `scratch/der-audit/`. They are not merged.

## Why strand-bend and strand-drop went NaN

- **strand-bend:** the bending force (finding 1) is non-conservative and lands on the wrong vertices. From step 1250 it folds the rod and drives the κb denominator toward zero (2.0, then 0.21 at step 2500, then 2.5e-4 at step 2960). The values become inf/NaN at step 2970. Halving or quartering dt does not save it, so this is not explicit-Euler instability.
- **strand-drop:** every spring has rest length 1 while the strands are spaced 0.25 apart (finding 3). Strain is 0.375 after step 1 and the run is NaN by step 30. Fixing finding 1 alone still dies at step 23.
- **Proven fix:** with findings 1 and 3 fixed in a scratch copy, bend stays finite through 7000 with a symmetric arch (mirror error about 1e-14). Drop stays finite through 20500 at 0.5% strain, and twist through 3000.

## Findings

| # | Where | Bug | Evidence | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `DiscreteElasticRod.cc` `UpdateKbGradients` and the bending scatter in `ComputeCenterlineForcesStraight` | `e1` is set to e⁰ instead of e¹. Only the outer product is divided by D. The `rhs` sign is flipped. Only one of the three ∇κb blocks is stored, and it is added to vertex `ii` instead of `ii+1`. The last two vertices get no bending force. | Blocks are 99-680% off. Net force on a free rod is 15.6, where it should be 0. The force is non-conservative. | **Fix.** Use the paper's three blocks: ∇_{i-1}κb_i = (2[e^i]× + κb_i e^{iT})/D and ∇_{i+1}κb_i = (2[e^{i-1}]× − κb_i e^{(i-1)T})/D, with D = \|ē^{i-1}\|\|ē^i\| + e^{i-1}·e^i. The middle block is the negative sum of the other two. Scatter onto all three vertices. |
| 2 | `UpdateHolonomyGradient` | It mixes κb_{i-1} with κb_i, multiplies by the length where it should divide, and never writes index 0, so that value is uninitialized. | 113-122% off FD. | **Fix.** Per vertex: ∇_{i-1}ψ_i = κb_i/(2\|ē^{i-1}\|) and ∇_{i+1}ψ_i = −κb_i/(2\|ē^i\|), with the middle vertex getting the negative sum. |
| 3 | `DiscreteElasticRod` constructor, `MassSpring(100000, 1)` | One spring with rest length 1 serves every edge. | At rest with 0.25 spacing the force is 8.3e4, where it should be 0. | **Fix.** Give each edge its own rest length. |
| 4 | twist contribution in `ComputeCenterlineForcesStraight` | The code uses βm/L. The gradient of E = βm²/L is 2βm/L. | The 2βm/L form matches FD at 2.4e-10. | **Fix.** |
| 5 | `AdaptedFrame.cc` and `RotationMatrixAroundNormal` | It rotates by \|κb\| = 2tan(φ/2) instead of the turning angle φ. | At φ = 1 it turns by 1.0926. | **Fix.** Use angle 2·atan(\|κb\|/2). |
| 6 | `frames[0]` | Set once in `Initialize` and never parallel-transported in time. | \|u⁰·t⁰\| = 7.6e-2 after a deformation. | **Fix.** Transport u⁰ onto the new t⁰ every step. |
| 7 | `ComputepEpTheta`, the general centerline path | Reads `curvature.at(nRods-1)` past the end, so it always throws. | | **Delete** (no callers). |
| 8 | `ComputeTwistingForce` | Multiplies terms that should add, and includes a term that does not depend on θ. | 100% off FD. | **Delete** (no callers). |
| 9 | `ComputeTwistingHessian` | Missing a J, uses the wrong length, and is not SPD. | 39% off FD, and one eigenvalue is −0.5. | **Delete** (no callers). |

## Kept on purpose (not bugs)

- **`mInv = 5`:** an inverse mass of 5, so mass is 0.2.
- **Velocity filter:** `I − dt·c·M⁻¹` is deliberate ad-hoc damping.
- **θ per interior vertex:** θ is stored at interior vertices, not per edge as in the paper.
- **Cubic spring:** `−|e|∇Ψ` is a conservative cubic spring (FD error 1.5e-9).
- **`MassSpring::Gradient`:** matches FD to 1.8e-9.

## Tests

- **`TestLibMath.FactorTriDiagonalMatrix`:** the test is wrong. Eigen's `ConjugateGradient` reads only the lower triangle and assumes a symmetric matrix, and the test feeds it random non-symmetric ones. Its one caller is finding 10, which gets deleted, so the function and the test go with it.
- **TestSNH and TestTetMesh:** both correct.
- **Strand coverage:** no test covered strand forces before this audit.
